Location: Hong Kong SAR

Completed: January 2024

This project documents my perception of Hong Kong as I experienced it. As per the title of the work, my perspective as a foreigner is not unacknowledged. I do not claim to be making any statements about the urban planning or green space of Hong Kong, but I aim to document it in this work, working to emphasize the role of color in making the city a vibrant and thriving place.
