Location: Tokyo, Japan

Completed: April 2025

How can the city be both a vessel and a veil? The character of a city is a product of the mass movement of its people, but to what extent is the individual an active agent in this process? My time in Tokyo was punctuated by these questions as I brushed up against a staunch social conservatism that contrasted my home of New York. Through this collection, I aim to investigate the mechanism by which Tokyo subsumes its residents and how their image manifests regardless.
