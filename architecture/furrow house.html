Completed: May 2024

For: ARCH 1104: Elective Design Studio for Non-Majors

Site: Seneca Lake

Carved into a rock face off the coast of Seneca Lake, this project aims to give form to a wildlife photographer’s strict regimen: drinking black cof- fee at dawn, taking photographs of the local fauna according to the position of the sun, developing and religiously cataloging each photograph, eating a daily late meal often shared with friends, clean- ing, and then ending the day by photographing the night sky.

The archaeological nature of this project came from the grain of cardboard, one of the few ma- terials we were tasked to use. Through material studies, I found the material apt for mimicking the erosion of geological forms.

With the studio largely focusing on analog meth- ods, I was challenged to use photography rather than renders to make viewers feel as though they are experiencing this project from the perspective of this fictional photographer.
